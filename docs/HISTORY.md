## Demma's Home

- **V1** (27 Oct 2026): a fresh start in its own repo (DIY's 5.2 GB history left behind): the app rebuilt house first (Home, House,
  Projects, Shop, Measure), the house file with every piece named and sourced, edit and measure in the app, the build and every check in
  the workflow, outputs built not committed. The client: "change the name to Demma's Home V1 and we will start from the beginning".
- **V1.1** (27 Oct 2026): realism, quality and speed from the living room's engine: CC0 photographed oak, hessian, corduroy, plaster and
  carpet over the drawn textures (each scaled to the part's own colour), rounded edges on every box, the iPhones' soft shade and glow when
  their frames are quick, the carpets' shared picture sent once. The client: "i want better realism, quality and speed".
- **V1.2** (28 Oct 2026): the old design back, as the client chose ("i really dislike this design and it doesnt look anything like my old
  one"): the media wall's page is the site again, opening on the whole house; the brand says where you are; the phone's floor and room
  pills one row each; Measure in the dock; the V1 app (its lists, menus and tab bar) retired.
- **V1.3** (28 Oct 2026): the house outside: always by day under a real sky, ground to a hazy horizon with the slope the terrace is built
  into, the sun moved to where the sky shows it (it was the living room's low one), the lawn green, the fences boarded, the cordyline from the back photo. The client: "continue upgrading the 3d model of the house".
- **V1.4** (28 Oct 2026): the far end and the view beyond, from the view photos: the end trees (a conifer, tall stems in leaf, next door's
  evergreen), the far fences, the low grey-roofed building past them, the valley's trees and the hill of houses opposite; the cordyline is
  next door's (over our fence). The client: "merge it now and continue".
- **V1.5** (28 Oct 2026): out of the living room's window by day, the modelled outside (garden, trees, valley, hill) in place of the
  plain sky, as the client's video frame through it. The client: "merge it and continue".
- **V1.6** (29 Oct 2026): Be there, in every room (standing eye height, a wide lens, look round); the gardens round ours (the table and
  chairs, next door's trampoline and sheds, their lawns and fences through the living room's window). The client: "give every room a wide
  angle within the room as if you're there viewing option".

  and the build's own icons out (33 files); a repo check against crowding; the outside's sun direction no longer a zero light in every living

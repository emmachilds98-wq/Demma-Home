# Storage and display (N212, redrawn by N223, 23 Nov 2026)

Emma: "keep storage in mind as its a strange shaped house and needs to be adequate for all our things and displays / knick nacks"
(21 Nov), then her brief (23 Nov): "I want storage ideas that are unique and don't take up lots of space so various small to medium
unless there's adequate space, it should be fit with similar design to the living room and office bedroom 3".

So each idea is small or medium, made for its spot, in the media wall's and the office's artisan oak (its ledge and lip, wedged
through-tenons, a face frame). Most are made to the spot from the media wall's oak board (`fit.SOURCES` "snowdonoak", £61.50 a board);
the cost is the oak it takes plus its fittings (`storage_ideas.oak`). Two are bought: the hall's peg rail (Willow & Stone) and B&Q's
oak floating shelf.

**Options only** (CLAUDE.md, core plan vs options): no build steps or buy rows until she picks one. In the 3D: tap a room's floor,
**More**, **Storage ideas**; tap a piece for its line. `python3 house/storage_ideas.py` prints the numbers; `house/check_model.py`
checks each piece fits (its room, clear of the house, the doors' swings, the slopes and the walks).

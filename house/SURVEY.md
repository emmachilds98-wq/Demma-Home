# The survey: measurements and photos for the whole-house model

For the client (Emma and Dave). Fill in what you can, in any order; send it a room at a time (a photo of this sheet with numbers
written on is fine). What the plans already say is filled in: just check it and correct it. Measure in centimetres.

**How to measure:** a tape (a laser measure is quicker), corner to corner at about waist height, along the skirting if furniture is in
the way. Heights from the floor. "Left" and "right" as you stand in the doorway looking in.

**How to photograph each room:** stand in each corner and take one photo of the opposite corner (four photos a room, landscape, the
ceiling and floor both in shot); one photo straight at each window and each door; one close-up of the floor and of anything special
(boxing, cupboards, the radiator). A tape measure stretched out in one or two of the photos helps us check the scale. Daylight, the main
light on. Name them by room if you can (e.g. "kitchen 1").
